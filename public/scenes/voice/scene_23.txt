Bird beats brute force. Fifteen meters of beak. Thirty percent less noise. That is the number that changed the argument. Not more power. Not thicker concrete. A softer pressure gradient. The train still compresses the air. It still makes a wave. But the wave arrives gently enough that the tunnel can swallow it. The invisible wall did not disappear. It was reshaped. And the same trick works wherever a fast object meets a narrow passage.
